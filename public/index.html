Questions

What people usually ask

Will the AI "fix" a wrong total and hide the problem?

No. It's told to copy numbers exactly as written. Separate code then checks the maths, so a wrong total shows up as a problem instead of disappearing.

Does it work with handwritten or photographed receipts?

Photos of printed receipts work well. Mostly handwritten documents are less reliable, so those are flagged for a person to check.

Where does the data go afterwards?

Wherever you work: a Google Sheet, your accounting software, a CRM or your own database. Records that need checking wait in a short review list.
